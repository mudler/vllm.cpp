## The defect

`compare_clock_records` bounds one cross-arm quantity:

    offset_pct = (ours_median / theirs_median - 1) * 100      # <= 1.0

`MAX_CROSS_ARM_OFFSET_PCT` exists because a systematic clock difference between
the arms transfers wholly into the ratio, at a physics ceiling of one point of
kernel time per point of clock. **The median is not the statistic that carries
that transfer.** Throughput is an integral over the window; what it sees is the
arm's MEAN clock. On the recorded evidence the two disagree, and the median reads
the cleanest number the gate can print while the means are apart:

| pairing | `median_offset_pct` | mean offset | ours mean cost | vLLM mean cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| c1 rep 1 | **+0.0000%** | **-0.2521%** | +0.1363% | -0.1161% |
| c1 rep 2 | **+0.0000%** | **-0.1530%** | +0.4020% | +0.2494% |
| c1 rep 3 | **+0.0000%** | **+0.1035%** | +0.2040% | +0.3071% |
| the three legs folded per arm | **+0.0000%** | **-0.1007%** | +0.2474% | +0.1468% |

Mean cost is `(median - mean) / median * 100` over the retained busy series,
**signed**: positive means the mean sits BELOW the median, which is what a
downward excursion population does.

**Why the median cannot see it.** `clock-gate-route.md` Finding 1 established
that the excursions are generated by the measured workload, once per request, at
each arm's own per-request period: over the nine windows there are 40 inter-burst
gaps, 35 of them in 28.39-35.48 s, and the trains carry the request trains' own
irregularity. Two arms with different throughput therefore take different numbers
of excursions over the same wall time. That population is 5.74% of samples and
sits almost entirely below the median — of the 97 labelled samples, 95 are below
their window's median — so a median over 155 to 246 samples steps straight over
it. It is also precisely the part that does NOT cancel between the arms, and
therefore precisely the part that transfers.

**Re-derived for this spec**, not read out of `7e07bbc91` or out of
`clock-gate-route.md`: every number above and in §Evidence was recomputed from
the raw `*.samples.json` at `/mnt/nas_share/rc/q38bf16/out/`. The re-derivation
reproduces the committed per-leg table exactly on all nine windows and every
column, including the 1690 retained busy samples, the 97 labelled ones (5.74%),
the two-state census (2489 MHz 826 samples, 2515 MHz 713, together 91.07% over 52
distinct values) and the per-leg mean costs.

## Design

    MAX_CROSS_ARM_MEAN_OFFSET_PCT = 1.0   # (ours_mean / theirs_mean - 1) * 100

One term, one number, and the number is not a new one.

### The statistic: the cross-arm MEAN offset, not a difference of burdens

#1546 proposes "one additive term holding the two arms' mean-clock cost within
the same physics ceiling". Read literally that is a bound on
`| |cost_ours| - |cost_theirs| |`, the difference of the two arms' excursion
burdens, which is the quantity the issue quotes at 0.020 / 0.153 / 0.103 points
rep for rep. **This row bounds the mean offset instead, and the difference is not
cosmetic.** Two constructed pairings, both recomputed for this spec, separate the
three candidates. Every window is n=155. In case C both arms have a median of exactly 2489 MHz, so
`median_offset_pct` is exactly 0.0000%; both arms' within-run spread is 3.5757%,
under the 5.0% ceiling, and no throttle bit is set, so **no existing rule fires
at all** and the reason list is empty before the change:

| case | ours | vLLM | ours cost | vLLM cost | difference of burdens | signed difference | **mean offset** |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| C, anti-symmetric | 105@2489 + 50@2400 | 105@2489 + 50@2578 | +1.1535% | -1.1535% | **0.0000** | 2.3069 | **-2.2806%** |
| D, compensating | 78@2489 + 77@2515 | 77@2489 + 78@2515 | -0.5189% | +0.5136% | 0.0054 | **1.0325** | **-0.0067%** |

- **Case C kills the difference of burdens.** Both arms carry an identical
  excursion burden in magnitude and opposite in direction, so the statistic the
  issue quotes reads **exactly 0.0000** while the two arms' mean clocks are
  **2.28% apart**. An absolute value discards the sign, and the sign is the whole
  of the transfer: an upward excursion on one arm and a downward one on the other
  ADD, they do not cancel. A gate built on that statistic passes a pairing biased
  by 2.28%.
- **Case D kills the signed difference.** `cost_ours - cost_theirs` reads
  **1.0325** on a pairing whose mean clocks are **0.0067% apart** — nothing
  transfers at all. The arms simply sat on opposite sides of this box's own
  two boost states and their medians snapped to different ones. Case D is not a
  defect that needs catching either: `median_offset_pct` reads **-1.0338%** and
  the EXISTING rule already refuses it, at 1.03 against its 1.0 ceiling.

So the two candidate difference statistics are wrong in opposite directions: one
is blind where the transfer is largest, the other loud where there is none. The
mean offset is right on both, for the reason it is right in general — **it is the
quantity that transfers**, and it is the same quantity `MAX_CROSS_ARM_OFFSET_PCT`
was reaching for when it took the median as a proxy for it.

The difference of burdens is not discarded. It is **reported** beside the term,
per arm and folded, so a reader can see WHICH part of the distribution carried a
refusal. It is reported and never gated on, the same demotion this module already
applies to `CLOCK_TIME_TRANSFER` and to `spread_pct`'s successor.

### The threshold: 1.0, and it is the ceiling that is already here

**The forward criterion, unchanged from `bench-assert-clock-state.md`.** For any
kernel whose time scales with clock the transfer is bounded above by 1.0 point of
time per point of clock — a 12.79% clock deficit can cost at most 12.79% of time
— so a 1.0% mean offset implies AT MOST a 1.0% effect on the ratio, with no
appeal to any measurement. That is under the **2.97%** smallest deficit this
harness has been used to rank, so a pair inside the threshold cannot have had its
ranking inverted by clocks. **This is the identical argument, applied to the
identical kind of quantity — a systematic cross-arm clock difference — carried by
a different part of the distribution.** No new number is introduced and none is
fitted.

**The two-sided bound, because a threshold that only rejects is untested.** What
must the ceiling ACCEPT? Not much, and that is the point: two arms whose MEAN
clocks are more than 1% apart did not run at the same clock, whatever produced
the difference. The must-accept side is therefore already settled by the existing
rule — a pairing whose arms sit at this box's two different steady boost states,
2515 and 2489 MHz, is 1.0446% apart and is ALREADY refused by
`MAX_CROSS_ARM_OFFSET_PCT` at the same 1.0 ceiling (case D above measures
-1.0338% on the median). A mean ceiling at 1.0 therefore admits exactly what the
median ceiling admits and adds no new class of refusal on steady windows. On flat
windows the two terms are **identical by construction**: mean equals median, so
every fixture and every recorded pinned window scores the same on both.

**What the term reads on real data.** 0.2521 / 0.1530 / 0.1035 on the three
recorded pairings and 0.1007 on the folded arms — about 4x below the ceiling,
**reported and not used to place it**. The load: Qwen3.8-27B bf16 at
`Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B` revision `1d4bf0f2`, 1024 input / 128 output tokens,
concurrency 1, three repetitions per arm, on `dgx:gpu0` (NVIDIA GB10, driver
580.173.02, applications clock 2418 MHz, maximum SM clock 3003 MHz, persistence
`Enabled`), boot `3fd9745a-d25a-426c-ba3c-97c958a85515`, UNPINNED, inside an `rc`
lease, 2026-08-19, sampled at `--interval 1` for a measured cadence of
1.094-1.100 s.

**Say the weakness plainly.** 2.97% is the smallest deficit ranked in #543 and is
`n = 1`. The 1.0 point-per-point transfer ceiling is a physics bound and needs no
measurement, but the criterion that 1.0 is "small enough" inherits 2.97 from one
comparison. That weakness is inherited, not added: the same number places
`MAX_CROSS_ARM_OFFSET_PCT`, and this row deliberately does not introduce a second
number that could drift from it. What would replace the inheritance is a measured
transfer coefficient at `n > 1` — `CLOCK_TIME_TRANSFER` is `n = 1` today and the
module says so — and, for the excursion population specifically, the per-request
markers Route C2 and
[#1386](https://github.com/mudler/vllm.cpp/issues/1386) ask for.

### A cross-arm term is not a per-arm term, and conflating them is the trap

The pair that makes the distinction executable, both arms n=155, median 2489:

| case | ours | vLLM | mean offset | verdict |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| A | 105@2489 + 50@2400 | 105@2489 + 50@2400 | **+0.0000%** | **established** |
| B | 105@2489 + 50@2400 | 155@2489 | **-1.1535%** | **refused** |

In case A each arm carries a 1.1535% excursion burden and the term is **silent**,
because a burden both arms pay cancels out of the ratio. In case B the same
burden on one arm alone refuses. That is the whole content of "cross-arm
symmetry", and it is why this term cannot substitute for a per-arm excursion
bound: case A is a window that both arms were held back in, which is Route B's
`MAX_EXCURSION_MEAN_COST_PCT` to catch, under #1354, and is not this row's to
add. Adding it here would be a second, unratified within-arm ceiling landing
under the wrong issue.

### Absence of the field is a refusal, not a skip

`summarize_sm_clocks` gains `mean`. Every record written after this change
carries it; **every record written before it does not**, including the nine
archived windows and every evidence tree captured to date.

A record whose mean is absent is a record this term cannot evaluate. It is
refused, naming the field and saying the record predates the term. The
alternative — skipping the term when the field is missing — is the failure this
repository has recorded more than once: an absent hook that looks like an armed
instrument, and a gate that prints a clean verdict on a quantity nothing
examined. `AGENTS.md` states the polarity directly: unknown is not absence or
success.

**Its blast radius, measured rather than asserted.** The refusal lands on
archived records only, and it changes no verdict:

- The nine 2026-08-19 windows already carry two independent refusals each. They
  gain a third reason and stay `DISCARD`. Their mean offsets, recomputed from the
  raw samples above, are 0.2521 / 0.1530 / 0.1035 — **the term itself is silent
  on all three**, and the added reason is the missing field, never the
  asymmetry. Nothing here re-scores them in either direction.
- No committed record in the tree carries a clock summary: the fixtures in
  `tests/tools/` are all built through `build_clock_record`, so they acquire the
  field automatically.
- The one `established` cell that predates this change, the 2026-08-15 pinned c4
  pairing at `.agents/model-matrix.md:94`, was measured over the retired host and
  `flock` path with evidence at `dgx:~/fix931/out_noping_run1/`, is not in this
  tree, and is not re-derived by any gate here. Its window was **flat at 2184 MHz
  over n=861**, where mean equals median and the term is identically zero, so
  re-recording it would not move it. It is not resurrected by this row and not
  voided by it.

`validate_clock_record` and `_REQUIRED_SUMMARY_FIELDS` are deliberately NOT
extended. Requiring `mean` there would make the PER-ARM verdict refuse archived
records too, which is a wider change than #1546 asks for and belongs with
whichever row makes the field mandatory in the schema. The refusal is scoped to
the one term that needs the field.

### The fold

`merge_clock_records` folds an arm's legs. The merged mean is the
**sample-count-weighted** mean of the leg means, which is exact — it is the mean
over the arm's whole retained busy series. It is not the unweighted mean of the
leg means, which is wrong whenever the legs differ in length, and the legs do:
155 / 155 / 156 on our arm and 245 / 246 / 244 at c8. A leg whose mean is absent
makes the merged mean absent rather than fabricated, so the refusal above
propagates instead of being papered over.

## Is this the forbidden shape?

`AGENTS.md`: *"Never make a red gate green by deleting an assertion or widening
its scope."* This change is the exact opposite and the property is mechanical
rather than argued.

- Every existing condition in `clock_reasons` and `compare_clock_records` is
  byte-identical after the change. No threshold moves, no comparison operator
  changes, no branch is removed. The diff appends.
- `reasons` is only ever appended to, so for any pair of records the new reason
  list is a **superset** of the old one.
- §Tests pins that superset property executably over a corpus that includes the
  nine real records, rather than leaving it to inspection.

The one thing to be honest about: a term that only refuses can refuse something
it should not. §Design's two-sided bound and case D are where that is argued, and
the answer is that on steady windows the new term and the existing one are the
same number, so the class of newly-refused windows is exactly the class where the
mean and the median disagree by more than 1% — which is the class the term
exists for.

## Interaction with the observation floor and the other rules

`clock-gate-route.md` established that `MIN_BUSY_SAMPLES = 30` at the measured
1.097 s cadence demands about **32.9 s** of retained busy samples against an
excursion period of 28.39-35.48 s, so the observation floor and the 5% spread
rule are already mutually exclusive on this workload. **A third rule must not be
unsatisfiable in combination with those two**, and this one is not:

- **It is not a within-arm rule at all.** It reads the two arms' means and
  nothing else, so a window that satisfies the floor satisfies this term whenever
  the other arm looks like it. Nothing about window LENGTH enters the statistic:
  unlike `spread_pct`, whose numerator is non-decreasing in `n` by construction,
  a mean-of-means is scale-free.
- **At the floor it is satisfiable, and the margin is measured.** The deepest
  sample in the whole campaign is 1859 MHz, 25.31% below its window's median. One
  such sample inside a 30-sample window moves that window's mean by **0.844**
  points. So an arm that catches one deep excursion the other arm misses, at the
  smallest admissible window, reads 0.844% and passes. Two such, and it reads
  1.687% and refuses — correctly, because a 1.7% mean-clock difference between the
  arms is 1.7% of the ratio.
- **At real window lengths there is no interaction to speak of.** The recorded
  legs are 155 to 246 samples, where one 25.31%-deep sample is worth 0.163 to
  0.103 points. The recorded pairings read 0.10 to 0.25.
- **Sliding a 30-sample window over the real legs is NOT how the term is
  evaluated, and the number it produces is not a bound on it.** Over all such
  sub-windows the extreme mean costs span -0.348% to +1.429%, so an adversarially
  chosen ours-window against an adversarially chosen vLLM-window reaches 1.71
  points. That is post-hoc selection on the statistic under gate, which
  `clock-gate-route.md` already refuses: the whole-leg window is the record,
  because a leg's throughput number covers the whole leg. The figure is recorded
  here so a later reader does not rediscover it and mistake it for a defect.

## What this does NOT do

- **It does not unblock the c1 ratio owed by
  [#915](https://github.com/mudler/vllm.cpp/issues/915) and
  [#979](https://github.com/mudler/vllm.cpp/issues/979).** The throttle rule
  refuses all nine windows independently, and a stricter term cannot make a
  refused pairing pass. Route A, the capability grant under #1354, is the only
  route with a demonstrated pass on this workload.
- **It does not retroactively validate any discarded window.** A number the clock
  gate discarded stays discarded. This change moves verdicts in one direction
  only.
- **It does not implement Route B.** The within-arm spread statistic is still
  `(max - min) / median` and is still wrong on its own terms. #1354 owns that.

## Risks

- **The mean bounds an INTEGRAL metric and not a phase-scoped one.** This is
  `clock-gate-route.md`'s third risk, carried here because it applies to the mean
  offset exactly as it applies to Route B's mean cost: the excursions are
  phase-locked to the request head, so the term bounds output throughput and
  median inter-token latency, and it does NOT bound time-to-first-token, whose
  own repeat CV is 2.20% against 0.01% for median inter-token latency in the same
  files. The verdict is still one verdict for the whole record; making it
  metric-class-scoped is Route C2 and needs a schema change.
- **The mean is an integral over a 1.097 s sampler.** A sub-second excursion is
  invisible to it, so the bound is over what was observed and not necessarily
  over the physics.
- **`n = 3`.** Every pairing figure here is three legs of one workload on one
  boot on one box.
- **2.97% is inherited and is `n = 1`.** Named in §Design; the row chooses to
  inherit rather than introduce a second number.
- **Archived evidence trees stop summarizing clean.** §Design measures the blast
  radius: no verdict changes, but anyone re-running `online_gate_summary.py` over
  a pre-change evidence tree gets one refusal per arm naming the missing field.
  That is the intended polarity and it is stated here so it is not a surprise.

## Tests

2. **The asymmetric pairing refuses, and it is RED before the change.** Case B:

3. **The anti-symmetric pairing refuses.** Case C. Asserts the number the

   executable rather than argued.
4. **The compensating pairing is refused by the EXISTING rule and not by this

   surviving reason names the median. This is the case that would be red if the
   signed difference of burdens had been chosen, and it must stay green.
5. **A record without a mean refuses, per arm and by name.** Both arms, one at a
   time, with the reason naming the field and the arm.
6. **The fold is sample-count weighted.** Legs of unequal length whose unweighted
   mean of means differs from the weighted one; the assertion pins the weighted
   value. A leg with no mean makes the merged mean absent.
7. **The reported fields are reported and not gated.** The per-arm mean, the
   per-arm mean cost and the difference of burdens appear in the comparison
   block; no gate expression reads them, which the mutation set proves.
8. **On a flat window the two cross-arm terms coincide.** Asserts mean offset
   equals median offset exactly for flat fixtures, which is what makes every
   pre-existing case behave identically.
9. **The superset property**, run at implementation time and recorded in
   §Evidence rather than committed as a case. Over a corpus of pairings — the
   nine real records with their means restored from the raw samples, plus every
   synthetic case above and the existing fixtures — the new reason list must
   contain every reason the PRE-CHANGE module produced. It is not a committed
   test because it needs the module at the base SHA, which the merged tree no
   longer holds; extracting it with `git show <base>:tools/bench/gpu_clock_state.py`
   is what makes the check executable, and the corpus reaches the NAS, which CI
   does not.

**Threshold mutation set.** `MAX_CROSS_ARM_MEAN_OFFSET_PCT` moved in the passing
direction must turn case 2 red; moved in the refusing direction it must turn case

**The report.** `tools/bench/online_gate_summary.py` prints the mean line beside
