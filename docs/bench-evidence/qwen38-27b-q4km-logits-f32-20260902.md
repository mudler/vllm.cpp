## The two gaps the predecessor could not vouch for are the two that resolved

The logit-delta evidence closed by refusing to promise a pass:

> Two of the six contested gaps (0.027185 and 0.058050) are below the
> f32-vs-oracle agreement this run measures at those steps, so widening the head
> is necessary, not obviously sufficient.

**Those are exactly the two prompts that became token-exact**, and no other did.
The caution was right to be stated and the outcome fell the other way, which is
worth recording precisely because the prediction was pre-committed.

The three that remain -- 0.085434, 0.115482, 0.178236 -- are the three largest
FIRST-DIVERGENCE margins, and each still loses at the same index to the same
token as before. (Of all six recorded margins, sorted 0.027185, 0.058050,
0.085434, 0.115482, 0.124247, 0.178236, the two that resolved are the two
smallest. 0.124247 is `p1/35`, a step INSIDE a prompt that has already diverged
at 34, so it is not a first divergence and no arm can be scored on it.)
Read together with the resolution finding, that says what remains is no longer a
resolution defect: at 0.0625 f32 resolution is no longer the binding constraint
at these magnitudes, so a step we still lose by 0.085 to 0.178 is a step where
our residual error genuinely exceeds the margin. The remaining term is one of
MAGNITUDE, and the next hypothesis belongs there rather than in another dtype
width.

**The prefix length is unchanged on all three.** Prompts 1, 2 and 4 agree with
the oracle for exactly as many tokens as before and then lose the same token to
the same competitor, so this change perturbed nothing upstream of those steps.

## What this does not establish, and what stays owed

- **The gate does not pass**, and this document does not argue that it nearly
  does. 3 of 6 is a failing gate.
- **The heavy tail is unexplained and untouched.** The predecessor measured our
  `max_abs` reaching 17.1606 against the oracle's 1.3657 at `p5/2`, `p5/11` and
  `p0/45` -- not temporal, flipping no argmax. Nothing in this change addresses
  it and it is not re-measured here. It stays owed under
  [#2534](https://github.com/mudler/vllm.cpp/issues/2534) and in the spec's
  `## Owed`.
- **Only the CPU tier is measured.** The change is tier-neutral by construction
  rather than by argument: the keep-quant GEMM branches on
  `out.dtype == DType::kF32` on all three tiers already
  (`src/vt/cpu/cpu_quant_gemm.cpp::StoreOutF32`,
  `src/vt/cuda/cuda_quant_dot.cu::MatmulBTQuantKernelCuda`,
  `src/vt/rocm/rocm_grouped_gemm.hip::MatmulBTQuantKernelRocm`). The ROCm arm
  could not be scored: its token gate returned `NOT_MEASURABLE`
  ([#2511](https://github.com/mudler/vllm.cpp/issues/2511)).
- **No throughput or memory figure is quoted from either arm**, including the
  ones the harness printed as a by-product. An ungated arm's throughput ranks
  nothing.
